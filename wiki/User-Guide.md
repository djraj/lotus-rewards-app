# User Guide

For members. Everything here works on phone and desktop — it's one website,
no app to install.

## Signing in

New sign‑ups default to a regular member (`role: user`). There are three ways in:

- **Email + password.** Choose *Sign up*, enter a name, email, and a password
  of at least 6 characters. You must **confirm via the email** before your
  first sign‑in (check spam).
- **Magic link.** Enter your email and choose *Send Magic Link*. You get an
  email with both a **6–8 digit code** and a **sign‑in link** — enter the code,
  or just click the link.
- **Forgot password.** On the sign‑in screen, *Forgot password?* sends a reset
  email; the link opens a "Choose a new password" screen.

Your session persists across reloads and browser restarts until you sign out
(control at the top‑right of the nav).

## Dashboard

- **Lotus Points** and **Completed Tasks** (your approved submissions).
- A **daily quote** that rotates by date.
- **Recent Activity** — your last five submissions with a status badge.
  Click an **Ongoing** row to open its proof dialog and finish it.
- **Achieve Your Potential** — progress toward the cheapest reward you can't
  yet afford.

## Earning points

1. **Tasks** screen — browse by category (Referral, Service, Content,
   Coordination) or search. Each card shows the points it's worth.
2. **Start Task** creates an *ongoing* draft and drops you on the Dashboard.
   You can only have one ongoing draft per task.
3. Open the draft from the Dashboard's Recent Activity. Add a **proof photo**
   (take one or upload; images only, max 10 MB — it's compressed before
   upload) and an optional **note for the reviewer**.
4. **Save Draft** keeps it editable and still *ongoing*. **Submit for Review**
   (enabled once a photo is attached) sends it to the admins — status becomes
   *Pending*.
5. When an admin **approves** it, the points are added to your balance and the
   activity shows *Approved* with `+points`. If it's **rejected**, no points
   are awarded.

Tasks are repeatable — after approval you can start the same task again.

## Redeeming rewards

1. **Rewards** screen — browse by category (Products, Sessions, Workshops,
   Reviews) or search. Cards show the cost and whether you can afford it
   ("Need N more" if not, "Out of Stock" if unavailable).
2. **Redeem Reward** opens a confirmation showing the cost and your balance
   after.
3. **Confirm** sends a **redemption request** for admin approval. Your points
   are **not** deducted yet — they come off only when an admin approves.
4. While a request is pending the reward shows **Requested** and can't be
   requested again. Track it under **My Redemption Requests**
   (Pending approval / Approved / Declined). A declined reward can be
   requested again.

## Status glossary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ongoing** | Task started, not yet submitted — a private draft only you can see |
| **Pending** | Submitted (or reward requested); waiting on an admin decision |
| **Approved** | Points added (task) or reward granted and points deducted |
| **Rejected / Declined** | No points awarded / no reward |

## Emails you'll get

Task started, task submitted, task approved, redemption approved, and reward
gifted — plus sign‑up confirmation, magic link, and password reset. See
[Email Notifications](Email-Notifications.md).
